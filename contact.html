Help

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real store?

No. ÉLVARÉ is a fictional demonstration website created by Virellion Apex. You can browse, add to bag and complete a simulated checkout, but no orders are fulfilled and no payment is ever taken.

How long does delivery take?

In this demonstration, standard delivery is shown as 5–7 business days, express as 2–3 business days and next-day delivery for orders placed before 1pm.

What is the returns policy?

The demonstration policy allows returns within 30 days of delivery for unworn items with original tags. See Shipping & Returns for full details.

How do I choose my size?

Open the size guide on any product page for body measurements in centimetres. Between sizes, we suggest sizing up for tailoring and down for knitwear.

Can I change or cancel an order?

In a live store, orders could be amended within one hour of purchase. In this demonstration, orders exist only in your browser and can be cleared at any time.

How is my information used?

Form entries, your bag and wishlist are stored only in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to a server. See our Privacy Policy.
