The House

Our Story

A fashion house founded on proportion, restraint and the belief that true luxury is felt before it is seen.

Philosophy

Less, considered more deeply.

ÉLVARÉ began with a simple conviction: a wardrobe should be built, not accumulated. Every collection is an exercise in editing — refining the silhouettes that matter until each one feels inevitable.

The name joins two ideas the house returns to in every season: élan, the ease of movement, and rare, the quiet distinction of things made with intent. Our clothes are designed to move with the body, to be worn for years and to grow more personal with time.

How we design

Three principles

I.

Proportion before ornament

We start with the line — where a shoulder sits, how a hem falls, the space between garment and body. Decoration is added only when it earns its place.

II.

Materials that move

Silk satin, double-faced wool, fine merino and supple leather are chosen for how they drape, age and catch the light — never for novelty alone.

III.

Made to be kept

Our collections build on one another season after season, so each new piece works with what you already own.

Craft & inspiration

Inspired by the atelier.

The house draws on the traditions of the tailoring atelier: precise pattern-cutting, careful finishing and garments considered from the inside out. Our design language is shaped by architecture — arched windows, plaster walls, and the way light travels across a room through the day.

That is why the ÉLVARÉ monogram sits within an arch: a reminder that every piece should frame the person wearing it, rather than the other way around.

“Modern luxury is quiet. It is in the cut, the cloth and the confidence of the person wearing it.”
